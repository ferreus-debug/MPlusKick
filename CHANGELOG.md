# Changelog

## 0.6.0 (first public release, beta)
- Party kick cooldowns in the interrupt tracker: each player's interrupt (from class and spec) and whether it's ready. Inside keys they're estimated from timing and marked `~`.
- Out-of-range fade for lit panel tiles.
- Raid markers on panel tiles and alert cards.
- Right-click spells in the interrupt details to mark them important or not (`/mk important`).
- Run recap when a key ends, plus history of the last 10 runs (`/mk runs`).
- Casting panel shown only in dungeons (optional).
- Updated for patch 12.1.5, including the damage meter's hidden player IDs.

## 0.4.0
- Interrupt tracker synced from Blizzard's damage meter, with per-player spell breakdowns.
- Health bars on panel tiles and alert cards.

## 0.3.0
- Casting panel with hover-to-select tiles. Resizable panel and alert cards.

## 0.2.0
- Options window, center alert cards, nameplate KICK tags, uninterruptible-cast filtering, and hiding alerts while the kick is on cooldown.

## 0.1.0
- One-key focus/mouseover/target kick and the Season 2 must-kick list.
